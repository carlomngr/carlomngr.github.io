Hey there 👋

I’m a 29-year-old guy who somehow turned his passion for computers, puzzles, and late-night problem solving into a career in cybersecurity. 

For the past 6 years I’ve been working mostly in Digital Forensics & Incident Response (DFIR), which basically means I spend a lot of time figuring out what went wrong, how it went wrong, and how to make sure it doesn’t happen again.

I studied Computer Engineering at [Politecnico di Torino](https://www.polito.it/), and since then I’ve been diving deeper into both the defensive and offensive sides of cybersecurity. I love switching hats: one day I’m hunting threats, the next I’m breaking things.

Outside of work, I try to balance out all those hours in front of a screen with some real-world adventures. I enjoy being in nature (bonus points if it involves mountains 🏔️), cooking with friends, or planning trips around the world to explore new places and cultures. I work remotely most of the time, so you’ll usually find me in Turin, but my roots are in Abruzzo — which means I never miss a chance to escape back to the mountains and recharge.

If you’d like to reach out, feel free to use the contact details provided on this website.
