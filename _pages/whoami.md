Hey there 👋

I’m a 29-year-old who somehow turned a passion for computers, puzzles, and late-night problem solving into a career in cybersecurity.

I grew up in Abruzzo and later moved to Turin to study Computer Engineering at Politecnico di Torino. Since then, I’ve been diving deeper into both the defensive and offensive sides of cybersecurity. I enjoy switching hats: one day I’m hunting threats, the next I’m breaking things.

After university, I spent 6 months in the French Riviera where, besides working in a cyber threat intelligence research group, I got to live close to the sea for the first time and experience the beach lifestyle.

Now I’m back in Turin, where for the past 6 years I’ve been working mostly in Digital Forensics & Incident Response (DFIR) — which basically means figuring out what went wrong, how it went wrong, and how to make sure it doesn’t happen again.

Outside of work, I try to balance out all those hours in front of a screen with some real-world adventures. I love spending time in nature (bonus points if it involves mountains 🏔️), cooking with friends, going to concerts, and traveling the world to explore new places and cultures. I usually work remotely, so you’ll often find me in Turin — but since I’m originally from Abruzzo, I never miss a chance to escape back to the mountains and recharge.
